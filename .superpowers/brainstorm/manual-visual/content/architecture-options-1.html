VLM-first

Flexible for unfamiliar maps, but latency, determinism and GPU cost make it unsuitable for per-frame combat control.

Recommended control loop

CPU: capture, ROI extraction, OCR, tracking, state machine and safety checks. GPU: batched YOLO inference. VLM: event-triggered diagnosis or calibration, never the direct hot-path controller.
